# Observatory methodology

## Aim

Publish a neutral, evidence-led public record of the North Barnes Farm proposal and its potential landscape appearance.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Formal planning submissions and statutory consultation documents.
2. Lewes District Council / East Sussex County Council evidence and local-plan material.
3. Developer, landowner and appointed consultant material.
4. Established local reporting used only where primary documentation is not yet published.
5. Campaign material used as a lead or viewpoint, never as the sole basis for disputed factual claims.

## Visualisation rules

- Geography must be fixed from real coordinates before any aesthetic rendering.
- Site boundary area must be checked against the published figure.
- Existing roads, villages, railways, watercourses and public rights of way must not be moved to improve composition.
- Housing density must be derived from the published dwelling total and developable land, not guessed visually.
- Green infrastructure must be represented where the developer/council concept includes it.
- Utilities, treatment works, substations, pumping stations, detailed junctions and roundabouts must not be shown as definite unless published.
- Every visualisation must state whether it is an official image, an evidence-based reconstruction, or a purely illustrative scenario.

## Change history

Material is retained rather than silently overwritten where possible. When the developer changes the proposal, the Observatory should preserve the previous version and record the date and source of the change.

## Public neutrality

The Observatory is neither promotional nor oppositional. It should avoid both developer gloss and campaign dramatization.
