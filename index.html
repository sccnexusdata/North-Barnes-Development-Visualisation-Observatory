History & news

How the proposal has evolved

2023

Early vision material

Developer concept material described a settlement of up to roughly 3,250 homes with extensive green infrastructure and community facilities.

2025

Local-plan assessment

Lewes District Council assessment material considered the site at roughly 184 hectares with an indicative capacity above 3,000 homes.

2026

Current consultation

The emerging proposal is now generally described as up to 3,000 homes with commercial, employment, school, health, sports and landscape infrastructure.

Sep 2026

Evidence-led visualisation project launched

This public record separates established geography, published proposal components and illustrative assumptions.

Evidence & provenance

Three confidence levels

Published / evidenced

Directly supported by developer, council or statutory material.

Indicative / under assessment

Required or proposed in principle, but final design or location remains unresolved.

Illustrative only

Analytical proxy used to understand possible scale and appearance.

No invented A27 junctions. No River Ouse through the site. No relocated villages. No fictional treatment works. Geography first, rendering second.
