Current proposal baseline

A new settlement, not just a housing estate

The current public baseline describes up to 3,000 homes with supporting school, health, employment, commercial, sports and green infrastructure. The exact engineering and final spatial layout remain unresolved.

History & news

How the proposal has evolved

2023

Early vision material

Developer concept material described a settlement of up to roughly 3,250 homes with extensive green infrastructure and community facilities.

2025

Local-plan assessment

Lewes District Council assessment material considered the site at roughly 184 hectares with an indicative capacity above 3,000 homes.

2026

Current proposal baseline

ECE Planning describes up to 3,000 homes with schools, green space and employment; Welbeck continues to describe a long-term mixed-use settlement delivered over 25 years.

Sep 2026

Visualisation Observatory

The public project adds reproducible phasing, source cross-references and progressively more constrained imagery.
