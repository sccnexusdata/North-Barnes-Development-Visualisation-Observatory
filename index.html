Timeline distinction

Welbeck Land describes a 25-year placemaking approach. An independent audit reports that EIA scoping material anticipates construction from 2029 to 2039, with secondary reporting repeating that window. Until the exact primary page is archived here, the Observatory publishes the distinction rather than pretending the two statements are the same thing.

History & planning context

How the proposal and evidence have evolved

2023

Early vision material

Historic developer concept material described a settlement of up to roughly 3,250 homes with green infrastructure and community facilities. It is retained for chronology, not treated as the current final layout.

2025

Site 11EC in council assessment

Lewes District Council planning-policy material identifies North Barnes Farm as Site 11EC, land between Plumpton Green and South Chailey, and records material landscape/transport constraints and assessment context.

2026

EIA scoping and current 3,000-home baseline

Planning reference LW/26/0191 concerns EIA scoping for a hybrid application of up to 3,000 homes with community/education facilities, employment and green/blue infrastructure. Current promoter material also identifies healthcare, sports and nature-recovery components in principle.

Sep 2026

Independent-review candidate

The Observatory adds live terrain, an illustrative proposal scale study, symmetric claim review, privacy/licensing, exact release hashes and a public verification route while retaining strict evidential blockers for exact geometry and photomontage.

Evidence & provenance

Two questions are kept separate: is the claim supported, and is the geometry exact?

Evidence status

Primary/authority support, secondary corroboration, campaign claims and unresolved issues are graded explicitly rather than collapsed into one scale.

Geometry status

Published, indicative and illustrative geometry are separate. A supported facility can still have an unresolved location.

Independent challenge

Promoter and campaign claims are both retained where material, but neither side's rhetoric is converted into fact without corroboration.

No invented A27 junctions. No relocated villages. No fictional rivers or treatment works. No campaign map is silently promoted to an official boundary. Unknown engineering remains unknown until evidence fixes it.
