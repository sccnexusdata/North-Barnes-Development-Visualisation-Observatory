1 · Release integrity

Verify the exact bytes being viewed

Release manifest

data/release.json records the exact private-repository source commit, release marker hash, SHA-256 hash of every approved public file and a deterministic tree fingerprint.

This allows a reviewer to distinguish "the current website" from an earlier cached or superseded version.

Repositories

Public Observatory repository contains the published site. The linked research repository contains methodology, source registers and the controlled public export where access permits.

Publication occurs through an explicit release gate rather than on every research commit.

2 · Claims

Promoter and campaign claims are graded under the same rules

The machine-readable claim review matrix records what is supported, what is merely reported, what comes from advocacy material and what still needs testing.

Currently corroborated

  • Eton College is identified by ECE Planning as landowner.
  • Welbeck Land is identified as a project/promotional partner.
  • The current baseline is up to 3,000 homes.
  • Lewes District Council material identifies North Barnes Farm as Site 11EC, land between Plumpton Green and South Chailey.
  • Welbeck Land describes a 25-year phased placemaking approach.

Open verification targets

  • The supplied independent audit reports a 2029–2039 construction window in EIA scoping material; exact primary bytes/page still need archiving in this project.
  • Campaign traffic figures, including millions of extra annual journeys, require reproduction from transport assumptions before use as facts.
  • Campaign claims about construction duration, water, sewage, flooding and ecology remain explicit test targets rather than accepted conclusions.
  • Reported 6,000 m² commercial and 10,000 m² light-industrial quantities remain pending direct primary-document confirmation.

3 · Geometry

Authority context is not the same thing as a verified red-line boundary

What is authoritative

Lewes District Council's 2025 LAA and Sustainability Appraisal identify the location as Site 11EC / North Barnes Farm. Existing roads, railway, settlements and watercourses are contextual mapping inputs.

Open the council Site 11EC assessment.

What remains illustrative

The yellow envelope, generated neighbourhood footprints, internal street guides and current 3D camera anchors are an analytical scale study. They are not a planning boundary, surveyed masterplan, final road layout or verified visual-impact camera.

The campaign's three published land maps are useful cross-checks, but are treated as advocacy/reference material until georeferenced and corroborated against authority/promoter geometry.

Presentation candidate ≠ evidential photomontage. The viewer is ready to be independently reviewed for clarity, provenance and technical behaviour. Forensic visual-impact claims remain blocked until exact source geometry, suitable terrain and verified observation cameras are locked.
