# North Barnes Development Visualisation Observatory

Public-facing evidence and visualisation site for the proposed North Barnes Farm new settlement in East Sussex.

## Purpose

This repository publishes a transparent, non-partisan record of the proposal as it develops: planning history, developer and council material, verified facts, news chronology, maps, aerial visualisations, landscape-view reconstructions and clearly-labelled assumptions.

The public site is designed to distinguish:

- **Published / evidenced** — supported by developer, council or statutory material.
- **Indicative / under assessment** — infrastructure or layouts that are expected or discussed but not yet fixed.
- **Illustrative visualisation** — reconstructed appearance based on published information; never presented as an official photomontage.

## Current baseline — September 2026

Published material describes a new settlement of up to approximately 3,000 homes with supporting commercial, employment, education, health, sports, landscape, drainage and transport infrastructure. Detailed engineering, utility locations and final road geometry remain subject to further assessment and planning submissions.

## Site structure

- `index.html` — public observatory homepage
- `styles.css` — site styling
- `data/timeline.json` — dated history and news chronology
- `data/evidence.json` — structured evidence register
- `images/` — generated and source-cleared visualisations
- `docs/` — methodology, caveats and source notes

## Relationship to research repository

The evidence-processing and geospatial build workflow is maintained separately in the research repository `sccnexusdata/North-Barnes-Development-Visualisation`. This public repository contains only publication-safe outputs and supporting provenance.

## Editorial standard

The Observatory does not campaign for or against the development. It aims to show what has been proposed, what is known, what remains uncertain, and what the development could plausibly look like at realistic scale.
